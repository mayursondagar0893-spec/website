📖 How to Use — Knowledge Hub

1

Select an author — Desktop

Click any name in the left sidebar. GST and Income Tax India are pinned at the top; all other authors are listed alphabetically. Use the search box to filter by name.

2

Select an author — Mobile

All authors appear as avatar chips in a horizontal scrollable strip below the top bar. Tap any chip to switch author instantly.

3

Search any article or topic

Type a word in the “Search articles, topics…” box (on mobile it sits just under the author strip). Matching links from all authors appear together. Tap an author name on a result to open that author, or tap “✕ Clear” to go back.

4

Author social icons

Quick-access social buttons appear below the author name. Tap any to open that profile in a new tab.

5

Browse links — List / Grid / Tab

Use the three toggle buttons (top-right of content area): ☰ List — full-width cards · ⊞ Grid — 2-column cards · ▤ Tab — one category at a time. Links are sorted alphabetically within each category.

6

Open a web link

Click or tap the olive title of any card (it ends with ↗). The link opens in a new tab.

7

Share a link

On any card, tap 💬 WhatsApp to send the title and link to a friend or client, or 🔗 Copy link to copy the link. On an author page and on search results, the same buttons share that whole page (they appear once this page is online).

8

Using the keyboard

Press Tab to move between items and Enter to open. In the author list and in category tabs, use the arrow keys. Press Esc to close this window or to clear a search.

9

Add authors or links (edit HTML)

Open in any text editor → find AUTHORS DATA. Authors and links are auto-sorted alphabetically on load.
